The licensed end product may be deployed to **one primary production domain**. Reasonable local development, private staging, disaster-recovery and backup copies for that same end product are permitted and do not count as separate end products. A domain migration or replacement is permitted when the prior production instance is retired.

The license is for one purchasing buyer entity and one end product. It is not a multi-client agency, multi-tenant SaaS, resale, white-label redistribution, or multiple independent stores license. A broader license requires a separate written agreement.
