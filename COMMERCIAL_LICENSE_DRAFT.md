## 2. Proposed buyer usage rights

After valid purchase, the buyer receives a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to download and retain one copy, install, run, study and modify the covered original materials, and use them to build and operate **one end product** for the buyer or one named client. Unless separately authorized in writing, the buyer may not resell, sublicense, rent, publish, share or redistribute the source package or substantial portions; offer it as a template, theme, boilerplate or competing downloadable product; use one purchase for multiple independent end products; remove applicable notices; or use third-party materials outside their licenses.

The licensor retains rights in original materials they own. The buyer retains rights in independently created content, data and branding to the extent permitted by law. ## 5. Support, defect fixes and external services

Purchase includes **30 days of basic installation and setup support** from the date of purchase. This support is limited to helping the buyer install and configure the unmodified product in a supported environment. It excludes custom development, hosting, domains, databases, email, payment processing, Cloudinary, AI/API usage, bespoke deployment work, ongoing maintenance, third-party service support, future updates and compatibility guarantees unless separately agreed in writing. For **30 days from purchase**, the seller proposes to correct reproducible defects in the unmodified delivered codebase (`dist/` and `backend/`) that prevent documented core storefront or checkout functionality from operating as specified. The buyer must provide sufficient steps and information to reproduce the defect. This commitment is limited to the delivered version and does not promise a particular resolution where a defect cannot reasonably be reproduced or is outside the covered materials.

Defect-fix coverage excludes issues caused by buyer code modifications, custom themes or plugins, unauthorized database schema changes, third-party API or service deprecations or breaking changes after delivery (including Paystack or Cloudinary changes), and unsupported hosting environments. It does not cover new features, major framework or runtime upgrades, or compatibility patches after the 30-day window. Those items require a separate written agreement or paid upgrade package. Nothing in this proposed policy limits mandatory statutory consumer rights.

The current demo checkout is not a promise of card-payment processing. Any payment provider requires separate configuration, integration and server-side verification.

## 6. Refund terms — proposed 14-day policy for legal and marketplace review

The seller proposes that buyers may submit a refund request within **14 calendar days of purchase** where the unmodified delivered software has a reproducible, documented defect that prevents documented core functionality and cannot be resolved within a reasonable timeframe, or where the delivered product is materially different from its listing description. Requests must be submitted through the marketplace or the seller's published support channel with sufficient details to assess the claim.

Mandatory marketplace refund rules and applicable non-waivable statutory consumer rights take precedence over any conflicting term in this draft. The proposed policy does not create an absolute bar to refunds after 14 days where mandatory law or marketplace rules provide otherwise.

Subject to mandatory law and marketplace rules, the seller proposes that the policy need not cover change-of-mind requests after the buyer has downloaded or accessed the source archive, lack of technical expertise to operate the documented Node.js/MongoDB/React stack, or issues caused by buyer code edits, customizations or unsupported third-party hosting environments. These exclusions must not restrict statutory rights or remedies.

If a refund is issued, the proposed condition is that the buyer's license to use the covered materials terminates and the buyer ceases use and deletes copies under their control, including deployed copies, to the extent legally permissible. Treatment of derivative works, backups and legally required retention must be confirmed by qualified legal review before publication.
